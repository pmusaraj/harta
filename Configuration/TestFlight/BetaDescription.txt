Harta is a maps and navigation app for iPhone and iPad. Search for destinations, save favourite places with custom names and icons, and get walking, cycling, or driving directions. Share your saved map with family members through iCloud: one person owns and edits the map, and invited members can view the same places.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Maps, search, and routing need an internet connection. Family sharing requires an active iCloud account for each participant. Harta is a prototype; offline routing and live traffic are not available.
