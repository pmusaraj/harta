This build adds two-way shared-map editing.

• Search for a place and try walking, cycling, and driving directions.
• Save a place or dropped pin. Touch and hold its name in the place details to rename it. Check that the name, icon, and colour survive closing and reopening the app.
• In Settings → Shared map, share your saved places with another person using their own iCloud account. Have them install this TestFlight build and open your invitation.
• Grant editing access in Manage my map’s sharing (existing invitations remain view-only until changed). Add, rename, customize, or remove places from both devices and verify changes reach the other. Also invite a view-only participant and confirm editing is disabled. Use Sync now if needed; automatic refresh runs about every 30 seconds while Harta is active.
• Switch between a joined shared map and personal saved places. Check that joining does not replace personal places.
• Edit offline, reconnect, and check that changes sync. Try simultaneous edits on different places. Downgrade an editor to view-only and verify pending changes are retained locally. Try removing a member or leaving a share, then refresh the other device.

Maps, search, and routing require internet access. Downloaded saved places can be viewed offline. Sharing requires iCloud; recent searches and live location are not shared. Navigation voice guidance starts muted. Please include the steps and a screenshot when reporting an issue through TestFlight.
